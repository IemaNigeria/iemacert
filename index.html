Accredited ISO Certification Services in Nigeria & Worldwide

IEMA Standards Limited (IEMACERT) is an internationally accredited Conformity Assessment Body (CAB 12203) providing management system auditing, industrial laboratory testing, non-destructive testing (NDT), technical inspection, and statutory regulatory compliance services across Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and international markets.

Core Management System Standards

  • ISO 9001:2015: Quality Management Systems (QMS) for customer satisfaction and operational excellence.
  • ISO 14001:2015: Environmental Management Systems (EMS) for statutory environmental compliance and resource efficiency.
  • ISO 45001:2018: Occupational Health and Safety Management Systems (OH&SMS) for workplace hazard prevention.
  • ISO/IEC 27001:2022: Information Security, Cybersecurity, and Privacy Protection Systems.
  • ISO 22000:2018: Food Safety Management Systems (FSMS) and HACCP principles for food supply chains.
  • ISO 37001:2016: Anti-Bribery Management Systems (ABMS) and corporate integrity governance.
  • EcoMark: Voluntary African & Global Sustainability and Green Product Certification Scheme.
